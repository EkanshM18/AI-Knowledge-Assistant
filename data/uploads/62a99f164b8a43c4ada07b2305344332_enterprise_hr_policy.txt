
ENTERPRISE HR POLICY MANUAL
Version: 3.2
Department: Human Resources

1. INTRODUCTION
This policy document outlines employee leave policies, attendance guidelines,
remote work eligibility, code of conduct, and employee benefits applicable to all
full-time employees of the organization.

2. LEAVE POLICY
Employees are entitled to the following leaves annually:
- 20 Paid Annual Leaves
- 12 Sick Leaves
- 5 Casual Leaves
- 10 Public Holidays

Unused paid leaves can be carried forward up to a maximum of 15 days.

2.1 Sick Leave
Employees must notify reporting managers within 2 hours of shift start.
Medical certificates are mandatory for leaves exceeding 3 consecutive days.

2.2 Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks
- Paternity Leave: 15 working days

3. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees are eligible for hybrid work after completion of probation period.
Remote work requests must be approved through the HRMS portal.

4. ATTENDANCE POLICY
Office working hours:
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST

Employees arriving later than 9:30 AM more than 3 times in a month may receive attendance warnings.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must:
- Maintain professionalism
- Protect confidential company information
- Avoid workplace harassment
- Follow cybersecurity guidelines

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Performance evaluations occur twice annually:
- Mid-Year Review
- Annual Review

Ratings:
- Outstanding
- Exceeds Expectations
- Meets Expectations
- Needs Improvement

7. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
Benefits include:
- Health insurance
- Life insurance
- Learning reimbursement
- Internet allowance for remote workers

8. ESCALATION MATRIX
HR Helpdesk: hr-support@company.com
L2 Escalation: hr-manager@company.com
